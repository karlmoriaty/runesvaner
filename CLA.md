# Runesvaner Contributor License Agreement

> **Status: EFFECTIVE since 2026-11-28.** Approved by the project Owner after legal review.
> Related decision: D-04 (ICLA + CCLA). The Owner is an individual, not an organization.

## 1. Purpose

This CLA allows the project Owner to accept contributions while keeping the project's licensing and governance sustainable. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions.

## 2. Definitions

- **Project**: the Runesvaner open-source project.
- **Owner**: the individual maintaining the project and holding its trademarks.
- **Contribution**: any original work of authorship submitted to the project, including code, documentation, tests, and design assets.
- **You**: the individual or legal entity submitting a Contribution.

## 3. Copyright license

You grant the Owner a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute your Contributions and derivative works.

You retain ownership of the copyright in your Contributions.

## 4. Patent license

You grant the Owner and recipients of the project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer your Contributions, where the license applies only to patent claims you own or control that are necessarily infringed by your Contributions alone or by combination with the project.

If you initiate patent litigation against the Owner or any recipient alleging that the project or a Contribution infringes a patent, the patent license granted to that party terminates as of the filing date.

## 5. Representations

You represent that:

1. You are legally entitled to grant the licenses above.
2. Each Contribution is your original work, or you have received sufficient rights to submit it under this CLA.
3. Your Contribution does not violate any third-party rights.
4. If your employer has rights to work you create, you have received permission to submit the Contribution, or your employer has signed a Corporate CLA.

## 6. No warranty

Contributions are provided "as is", without warranties of any kind, express or implied.

## 7. Notification

You agree to notify the Owner if you become aware of any facts or circumstances that would make these representations inaccurate.

## 8. Governing law and venue

The applicable governing law and venue are stated in the CLA signature record maintained by the Owner. The signature record is created when the first external contribution is accepted and is available to contributors on request.

## 9. Corporate CLA

The Corporate CLA (CCLA) uses the same terms for entities and is available from the Owner. An entity signs the CCLA once and lists the employees authorized to contribute under it.

## 10. How to sign

External contributors sign by commenting on their pull request:

> I have read the Runesvaner CLA (CLA.md) and I agree to its terms.

The Owner records the signature (GitHub username, date, pull-request link, governing-law entry) in the CLA signature record. A pull request is not merged until the signature is recorded. Corporate contributors sign the CCLA before their employees' first contribution.

Signatures are kept for as long as the project accepts contributions and are not used for any purpose other than recording the license grant.
